The Island of Nymoona

Off the coast of the Feywild lies a system of islands known as Nymoona. It's capital city is the well known port town, Fernstall. Fernstall is the center of all planar travel through a quartz-powered system of portals known as Breaches. While mostly peaceful, Nymoona often finds itself with a feud over royal power. Due to the concentration of magic on the island, royals are ridiculed often as many warriors possess similar power to that of the royals, only stunted by their lack of access to the royal scrolls.

Recent Events

After the encounter with the final living Draconic god, Flaksus, the Viscida Adventurers and members of the Butterfly Court Adventurers have reconvene Rubble to decide where to go from here. In the mean time, Giaco Heart, Atlas Axelo, and Brakudal search for V*Blor, prince of Xienon, in hope of eliminating him on their quest in the Thorn Isles off the eastern coast of Nymoona.
