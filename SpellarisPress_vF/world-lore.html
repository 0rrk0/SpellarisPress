World Lore

A collection of history, myths, and records from throughout the campaign world.

History

Long before the adventures of Portal Problems began, the world of Nymoona was shaped by powerful magic, ancient civilizations, and conflicts between beings of extraordinary power. Among these were demonic royalty from Xienon, the chromatic dragon lords of the age of flight, and fey royalty of the seasonal courts of the Feywild.

At the heart of these conflicts were ancient magical stones, powerful artifacts whose history became intertwined with the fate of the planes. As demonic forces began to threaten the boundaries between worlds, unstable portals appeared, allowing dangerous creatures and foreign powers to cross into places they did not belong.

What began as mysterious magical disturbances soon grew into a much greater conflict, drawing adventurers from across Nymoona into a struggle involving ancient powers, forgotten history, and the very fabric separating the planes of existence.

The true origins of the stones, the ambitions of Xienon's rulers, and Xirok's role in these events remain a central mystery of Portal Problems, with the adventurers uncovering more as the story comes to its conclusion.

The Feywild and Its Origins

Portal Problems takes place in a homebrew expansion of the Feywild, a magical plane of existence within the Dungeons & Dragons multiverse. While the island of Nymoona, its civilizations, and its draconic history are original creations, the campaign draws inspiration from the established lore of the Feywild.

For more information about the original setting, visit the official Dungeons & Dragons guide: Welcome to the Feywild: A Beginner's Guide

Magic

Magical Systems

Throughout the world of Portal Problems, several unique magical systems exist alongside traditional forms of magic. Two notable systems are Magical Tattoos and Soul Manipulation, both of which remain consistent across the events of the series.

Magical Tattoos

Magical Tattoos allow individuals to inscribe spells and abilities directly onto their bodies. These tattoos grant access to magical effects, provided the individual possesses the physical capacity to withstand and utilize them.

Soul Manipulation

Soul Manipulation is a rare and dangerous practice involving the exposure of an individual's soul to the material realm. Through magical or physical means, portions of the soul can be manifested and utilized in various ways.

The practice remains highly experimental and is typically associated with specific individuals rather than being a commonly understood or accessible form of magic.

Magical Minerals and Gemstones

Several varieties of magical gemstones exist throughout the world, each associated with a particular enhancement or ability. These minerals retain their established magical properties across the Portal Problems series.

  • Agris Ruby - Damage and offensive abilities.
  • Emberite Amber - Critical hits and luck.
  • Lumen Quartzite - Light and power.
  • Vitrim Emerald - Armor Class (AC) and defensive abilities.
  • Arcanion Sapphire - Spell enhancements and magical buffs.
  • Motus Amethyst - Movement and speed.

Ordinary, nonmagical varieties of these minerals can also be found. The magical variants retain the same fundamental effects and properties established in previous adventures.
